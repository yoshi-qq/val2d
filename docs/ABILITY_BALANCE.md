# Ability balance

The active balance targets standard PC matches through VALORANT patch 13.10. Scalars shared by the game and UI are
overridden in `config/patches/01_valorant_13_10.py`. Values embedded in effect recipes are in `config/content/agents/`.
Riot sometimes publishes distances in centimetres. Val2d uses metres.

Dark Cover targets a walkable surface by default. Ctrl+scroll adjusts the target in 0.25 m steps up to 3 m above
the surface. Unmodified scrolling selects the floor over a deck. Raised smoke descends at 5 m/s,
and its sight-blocking footprint expands on the selected floor as it descends. These values approximate the original.

| Agent | Patch-backed changes | Val2d adaptations still in use |
|---|---|---|
| Brimstone | Sky Smoke deploys in 1 s at up to 55 m, with a 4.15 m radius. Stim grants 10% fire rate and movement. Incendiary no longer damages players through solid walls. | Ground fire uses direct line of sight. Incendiary uses the existing top-down throw, and Orbital Strike keeps its existing footprint and continuous damage model. |
| Omen | Dark Cover equips in 0.6 s, locks weapon fire for 0.7 s after casting, and recharges in 40 s. Its orb travels at 64 m/s up to 80 m, then forms a smoke with a 4.1 m radius. The shadow view follows the smoke marker. Primary and secondary fire adjust distance, reload switches views, and the ability key casts. Paranoia equips in 1.1 s, travels 25 m at 20 m/s through walls with a 4.3 m radius and a forward spawn offset, and locks weapon fire for 0.7 s after casting. Shrouded Step equips in 0.5 s, teleports after a 0.7 s windup, and plays the arrival effect for 0.5 s. From the Shadows equips in 0.5 s and accepts targets across the map. After a 1.5 s windup, its destructible shade lasts 2.5 s and can be cancelled. A successful teleport starts the normal weapon draw. | Dark Cover uses discrete target heights. Range and flight time include the height difference between caster and target. Sound positions use the map plane. From the Shadows moves Omen at the start of the shade phase. The shade can collect the Spike and uses a sight radius that opens quadratically from zero to the map diagonal. Shrouded Step starts its 0.7 s weapon recovery on arrival, concurrently with the arrival effect. |
| Jett | One Updraft charge. Blade Storm applies the 0.85 leg multiplier. Holding jump while falling activates Drift unless Jett is suppressed or detained. | The Drift fall speed and dash travel use Val2d physics. Cloudburst still uses the top-down throw without steering. |
| Sova | Recon Bolt recharges in 50 s. The landed bolt has 20 HP and emits exactly two scans. | Recon and Shock bolts lack variable draw and bounce controls. Players steer Owl Drone, which still scans automatically. Bots use autonomous flight. Scan footprint, pulse presentation and Hunter's Fury beam geometry use Val2d's existing scale. |
| Sage | Barrier Orb costs 300 credits and has 15 m placement reach. Four segments start at 400 HP and fortify to 600 after 2 s. Slow Orb applies 50% movement and dash speed. Healing Orb targets injured teammates in sight and heals Sage for 100 HP over 5 s. Resurrection costs 7 points and reaches 12 m. | The barrier uses axis-aligned segments with the existing Val2d dimensions. Heal targeting uses a 0.75 m cursor tolerance. Resurrection uses a 2 s windup with no separate recovery animation. |
| Cypher | Trapwire has a 15 m length cap, 20 HP and a 0.7 s chain before its zap, or an early zap beyond 4 m. Spycam recharges in 45 s after destruction or 20 s after recall. Neural Theft costs 7 points, reaches 18 m and shows two fixed positions 4 s apart. | Trapwire still clips a line to nearby walls. Spycam is an automatic detector with aimed recall, without a camera view or manual darts. |
| Phoenix | Curveball winds up for 0.6 s. Run It Back costs 7 points. Self-healing from Blaze and Hot Hands continues after leaving the fire. | Blaze is a straight line. The steering, throw trajectory and flash-angle falloff use simplified Val2d rules. |
| Killjoy | Nanoswarm lasts 5 s and has 20 HP. Turret has 100 HP and a 100° cone, fires 50% faster than the previous Val2d cadence and recharges in 45 s after destruction. Turret and Alarmbot work within 40 m of Killjoy and take 2 s to reactivate. Alarmbot has 20 HP and pursues its target. Lockdown has 200 HP with the 0.15 s pulse delay. | The turret still combines a burst into one hit, Alarmbot uses a straight pursuit with wall collision, and Lockdown retains the existing top-down radius and movement restriction. |
| Breach | Aftershock extends 10 m past the wall and applies two 80-damage bursts. Fault Line starts 8 m ahead, extends 8–56 m depending on charge, and is 8 m wide. It winds up for 1.1 s, concusses for 2.5 s and recharges in 50 s. Flashpoint travels at 24 m/s and blinds for up to 2.25 s. Turning away shortens the flash. Rolling Thunder starts 8 m ahead and covers 32 m in eight blasts. It is 18 m wide, concusses for 4 s and costs 8 points. | Wall charges use the far face of the first solid wall. Aftershock uses a 6 m wide strip in the top-down view. Flashpoint scales linearly from 2.25 s head-on to 0.25 s at 90° or farther away from the flash direction. Knock-up uses the Val2d rules. |

The numbers in the last column are local adaptations. Riot's patch notes do not establish a complete set of
current dimensions, timings and collision rules for all abilities.
