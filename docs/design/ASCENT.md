# Ascent in Val2d

The playable map is [ascent.py](../../gameplay/data/maps/ascent.py). The tactical-map trace uses the official minimap projection (1656 px = 142.86 m). Each character in the grid covers one square metre. A digit indicates floor elevation. Stairs, doors and props are specified below the grid.

| Area | Elevation |
| --- | --- |
| A | A Main, Switch and Tree are at level 2. The front steps descend to the site at level 1. Garden is at level 3 and Heaven at level 4. |
| Mid | Courtyard and Link are at level 1. Catwalk, Cubby, Top and Bottom are at level 2. |
| B | B Site and Boathouse are at level 0. Lane, B Main and B Lobby are at level 1. Market and its landing are at level 2. |
| Spawns | Attacker spawn leads up to Mid Top. Defender spawn is at level 2. |

A and B site doors start open. Their site-side switches operate them with F. Closed doors block shots until destroyed. Shots and melee can also break A Window and the two Mid Link panels. Damaging abilities can hit the doors and panels using their blast, beam or persistent damage area. Thrown projectiles shatter the glass on contact. The sills at Mid Link and the open B Main windows block movement and allow sight and shots. Doors, glass and panels reset each round.

Heaven and Hell share the same footprint along the back of A Site. Heaven is at level 4 and Hell at level 1. Players reach Hell from the site floor, reach Heaven from the defender stairs, and can drop from the upper deck to the site. The ceiling separates movement, direct sight and gunfire between the layers. The top-down view cuts away the deck for players on the lower level.

The A Lobby recording shows level paving through the lobby and a short paved transition by the arch toward A Main. The B Lobby recording shows a nearly level entrance and shallow steps inside the hall. The map uses a one-level rise over 4 m at A and a half-level rise over 3 m at B. These dimensions are Val2d approximations inferred from the screenshots.
