# Ascent in Val2d

The playable map is [ascent.py](../../gameplay/data/maps/ascent.py). The tactical-map trace uses the official minimap projection (1656 px = 142.86 m). Each character in the grid covers one square metre. A digit indicates floor elevation. Stairs, doors and props are specified below the grid.

| Area | Elevation |
| --- | --- |
| A | A Main, Switch and Tree are at level 2. The front steps rise to the plant floor at level 3. Garden is at level 3 and Heaven at level 6. |
| Mid | Courtyard and Link are at level 1. Catwalk, Cubby, Top and Bottom are at level 2. |
| B | B Site and Boathouse are at level 0. Lane, B Main and B Lobby are at level 1. Market and its landing are at level 2. |
| Spawns | Attacker spawn leads up to Mid Top. Defender spawn is at level 2. |

A and B site doors start open. Use F on the site-side switch to close a door for the round. Closed doors block shots until destroyed. Shots and melee can also break A Window and the two Mid Link panels. Damaging abilities can hit the doors and panels using their blast, beam or persistent damage area. Thrown projectiles shatter the glass on contact. Mid Link's sills block movement. The panels below the two high B Main windows block ground-level movement, sight and gunfire. Doors, glass and panels reset each round. [Riot's map description](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/maps/#ascent) · [Riot's B Main comparison](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/news/game-updates/valorant-patch-notes-10-04/)

Heaven and Hell share the same footprint along the back of A Site. Heaven is at level 6 and Hell at level 3. Players reach Hell from the plant floor and Heaven from the defender stairs. Players can drop from the upper deck to the site. The ceiling blocks sight and gunfire across the slab. The view prioritizes the deck level of a visible opponent in the current viewport and defaults to the viewer's level. [A Site gameplay views](https://esportsdriven.com/valorant/guides/111/valorant-ascent-map-callouts-guide-with-images)

The A Lobby recording shows level paving through the lobby and a short paved transition by the arch toward A Main. The B Lobby recording shows a nearly level entrance and shallow steps inside the hall. The map uses a one-level rise over 4 m at A and a half-level rise over 3 m at B. These dimensions are Val2d approximations inferred from the screenshots.
