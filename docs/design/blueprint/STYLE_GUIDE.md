# Val2d style guide

As of 11.11.2026 · design session · applies to every sprite, drawn by hand or generated.
Templates for each sprite type are in this folder (see `README.md`); agent details are in `../CHARACTER_SHEETS.md`.
Decisions behind these rules are recorded in `docs/ENTSCHEIDUNGEN.md`.

## 1. Basics

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Scale | 16 px = 1 m. Agent 16 × 32, map tile 32 × 32 (= 2 m) |
| Native view | 480 × 270 px (30 m wide), shown ×4 at 1920 × 1080 |
| Scaling | Whole-number factors only, nearest neighbour, never smoothed |
| Perspective | Top-down map; figures drawn front-facing and upright, never rotated |
| Light | From the top left: light edge top and left, shadow edge bottom and right |
| Shading | Two steps per material (base, shadow). A highlight step only on effects |
| Outline | 1 px around figures and items, in a darker shade (about 45 %) of the colour it borders. No black outline. Tiles and flat UI have no outline |
| Darkest colour | about `#16151b`; never pure black |
| Colours | Figures ≤ 20 drawn colours; tiles 3–10; icons 1–3 |
| Originals only | No characters, logos, weapons, maps or UI from other games, even renamed or slightly changed |

Colours: `palette.png`. The neutrals have a slight blue-violet tint, as in the existing art.

## 2. Team and enemy marking

- Every sprite is **team-neutral**. Draw one version only.
- **Enemies** get a red outline (`#ee4c4a`), generated by `docs/design/check_agent_sprites.py` as `*_feind.png`. Later the colour becomes a player setting.
- Reserved colours: teal `#4fd6c8` (own team, UI accents) and red `#ee4c4a` (enemy, danger). Use them on sprites only as small accents.

## 3. Agents

Full rules in `../CHARACTER_SHEETS.md`. In short:

- 16 × 32 cell; the centre (8, 16) is the player position; boots end at row 30.
- Hit zones are drawn widths: head 8 px (columns 4–11), body 12 px (2–13), legs 7 px (5–11). Columns 0 and 15 stay free except for ability glow.
- 12 required states, including four of their own ability animations (`cast_c`, `cast_q`, `cast_e`, `cast_x`); everything else is optional with a fallback.
- Special mechanics: extra states `_mech_`, forms `@form`, overlays `_overlay_`.
- No weapons, muzzle flash or effects drawn on the figure; the game draws those.
- Every agent must be recognisable by silhouette alone: headgear or hair, shoulder shape and coat length matter most.

## 4. Weapons

- Side view, **muzzle pointing right**. In hand 16 × 8, buy menu 32 × 12 (with outline).
- Body in neutral greys (`#46454d` base, `#6c6b75` light top edge, `#232228` barrel and grip). **One accent colour** per weapon (stock, magazine or furniture) tells weapons apart.
- Read class by shape: pistols short without stock, SMGs with a wire stock and a straight magazine, shotguns long barrel without a magazine, rifles with a curved magazine, snipers with a scope and a long barrel, heavy weapons with a drum or belt.
- Grip and magazine stay in the gold zone of the template, so hands line up in every weapon.

## 5. Map tiles

- 32 × 32 = 2 × 2 m, seamless on all sides.
- Light edge top and left, shadow edge bottom and right (template edges).
- Walkable floor is mid-bright and low in contrast, so figures stand out on it.
- Walls are darker than the floor. Thin walls (penetration 0–1) show a lighter material with visible boards or panels, so players see that bullets go through.
- Crates and platforms have a clear frame so their height reads at a glance.
- Overlays (bomb site border) are transparent in the middle.
- Speckle and noise at most 16 pixels per tile; no busy patterns.

## 6. Effects

- Centre the effect in its cell; round effects fill the white ring of the template (the game scales them to the radius).
- Strips of 2–6 frames; the speed is listed in `README.md`.
- Effects may use a highlight step and partial transparency (alpha 90–240).
- Generic effects are white or light grey so the game can tint them per status or team. Agent-specific effects use the agent's glow colour.
- Effects that are repeated along a line (wall, wire, wave) tile seamlessly from left to right.
- Hits show sparks, no blood.

## 7. Interface

| Element | Size | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| Ability icon | 16 × 16 | white glyph (`#e8e6ef`) inside the 12 × 12 safe area, gold `#e0b437` for highlights; ultimate with a 1 px gold frame |
| HUD, status, minimap icons | 8 × 8 | one shape, 1–3 colours, readable at 1×; status icons use their status colour |
| Frames | 12 × 12 | nine-slice, 4 px corners; fill `#1f1e27`, edge `#0e0d12`, light edge `#2f2d3a`; selected with a teal edge |
| Bars | 48 × 6 | dark edge, upper half light and lower half shadow; stretched horizontally |
| Banners | height 21 | own 5 × 7 pixel font in capitals with a 1 px shadow, 12 px padding each side, accent line in the result colour (teal good, red bad, gold neutral) |
| Logo | 48 × 48 | crosshair logo on a rounded dark plate; also program icon at 16/32/48 |
| Player card | 64 × 128 | dark banner with a pointed bottom, logo at (8, 10), grey line at row 104 |
| Text | – | game font `font/fixed_sys.ttf`; all player-facing text in English |

## 8. Files and names

- File names stay as they are, even German ones (`wand_duenn.png`, `*_feind.png`); the code depends on them.
- Strips: frames side by side, no spacing, no padding.
- After drawing: enter status and file in `docs/SPRITES.md` and tell the coding session.
- Source files (`.aseprite`) sit next to the PNGs.

## 9. Check before handing over

1. Shown at 1× on the stone floor and on a dark wall: is it still readable?
2. Outline, light direction and shading follow sections 1–2?
3. Size and frame count match the template?
4. For agents, `python docs/design/check_agent_sprites.py <key>` runs without problems?
5. Original design, no borrowed look?
